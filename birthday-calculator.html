About the Birthday Calculator

Ever wondered exactly what day of the week you were born on, or how many days are left until your next birthday? Our free birthday calculator gives you all that information instantly. Just enter your birth date and discover your birthday details, including your zodiac sign and the exact number of days, hours, and minutes until your next celebration.

The calculator handles every case correctly, including people born on February 29 (leap day). It's a fun and useful tool for planning birthday parties, discovering interesting facts about your birth date, or simply satisfying your curiosity.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find out what day of the week I was born?

Enter your birth date in the calculator. It will instantly show the day of the week you were born, along with your next birthday information.

How is the next birthday calculated for February 29?

If you were born on February 29 and the current year is not a leap year, your next birthday is counted from March 1.

Does it show my zodiac sign?

Yes, based on your birth date, the calculator displays your Western zodiac sign.
