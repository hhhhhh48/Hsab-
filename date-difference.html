About the Date Difference Calculator

Our free date difference calculator tells you exactly how much time lies between two dates. Enter a start date and an end date, and instantly get the difference broken down into years, months, and days, along with useful totals like the number of weeks, hours, and minutes. You can also see the day of the week each date falls on and a count of business days only.

This tool is useful for many real-life situations: calculating your work tenure, the duration of a project, the time until an event, or the time between two historical dates. The calculator accounts for leap years and varying month lengths, so the result is always precise.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the date difference calculator work?

It subtracts the start date from the end date, accounting for leap years and varying month lengths, to give you the exact difference.

Can I calculate business days only?

Yes, the calculator shows the total number of weekdays between the two dates, excluding weekends.

Does it count weekends and holidays?

The total days calculation includes all days. The business days calculation excludes only weekends, not public holidays.
