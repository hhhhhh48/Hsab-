About the Age Calculator

Our free age calculator instantly tells you your exact age in years, months, and days. Simply enter your date of birth, and the calculator will show your age broken down precisely, along with useful details such as the total number of days, weeks, hours, and minutes you have lived. You can also see the day of the week you were born and a live countdown to your next birthday.

The calculator is completely free, requires no registration, and works on any device. Whether you need your age for a form, a visa application, or just curiosity, this tool gives you the precise answer in seconds.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is age calculated?

Age is calculated by finding the difference between your birth date and the current date, accounting for leap years and varying month lengths.

What about people born on February 29?

People born on February 29 are handled correctly. In non-leap years, their next birthday is counted from March 1.

Can I calculate my age at a future date?

Yes. Enter a future date in the "Age at Date" field to see how old you will be on that date.
