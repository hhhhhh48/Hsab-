About the Chronological Age Calculator

Chronological age is the exact time elapsed since a person's birth, usually expressed in years, months, and days. This calculator gives you the chronological age in the medical format (Y-M-D), which is commonly used by pediatricians, psychologists, educators, and speech therapists to assess a child's development.

To use it, simply enter the date of birth and the date of assessment (which could be today or any future date). The calculator will instantly display the chronological age in years-months-days format, along with the total number of months, weeks, and days. This is especially useful for school enrollment decisions and developmental milestone comparisons.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is chronological age?

Chronological age is the exact amount of time that has passed since a person's birth, typically expressed in years, months, and days.

Why is chronological age important in education?

Schools use chronological age to determine enrollment eligibility, grade placement, and to compare a child's development to age-appropriate milestones.

How is it different from corrected age?

Chronological age counts from the actual birth date. Corrected age (used for premature babies) subtracts the number of weeks the baby was born early.
