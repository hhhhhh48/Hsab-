When Can You Retire?

Retirement age varies dramatically around the world, and knowing your exact retirement date is the first step in financial planning.

Retirement Ages Around the World

In the United States, the full retirement age for Social Security is between 66 and 67, depending on your birth year. In France, the legal retirement age is 64. In Germany, it is 65-67. In Japan, it is 65. In Algeria, it is 60. Some countries allow early retirement with reduced benefits, while others incentivize working longer.

Planning Your Retirement Date

The first step is calculating exactly when you will reach your target retirement age. This lets you count the years, months, and days remaining until that milestone, and plan your savings accordingly.
