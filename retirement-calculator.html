About the Retirement Age Calculator

Planning for retirement starts with knowing exactly when you can retire. Our free retirement calculator tells you the precise date you will reach your target retirement age, along with the exact number of years, months, and days remaining until that day. Whether your country's retirement age is 60, 65, or 67, you can customize the target age to match your situation.

This tool is useful for financial planning, deciding when to start saving, or simply visualizing how much time is left before you stop working. It also works for planning early retirement if you want to retire before the standard age.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the retirement age calculated?

The calculator finds the exact date you will reach your chosen retirement age by adding your target age in years to your birth date.

What is the standard retirement age?

The standard retirement age varies by country. It is commonly 60-67 depending on your country's pension system.

Does the calculator consider early retirement?

Yes, you can enter any retirement age you choose, whether earlier or later than the standard.
