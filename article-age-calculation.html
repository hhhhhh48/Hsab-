How to Calculate Your Exact Age

Age seems simple: just subtract your birth year from the current year, right? Not quite. The exact calculation of age is more nuanced, and understanding it can reveal fascinating facts about time.

The Basic Formula

To calculate your exact age in years, months, and days, you subtract your birth date from the current date, then adjust for two things: months of different lengths and leap years. This is why two people born in the same year can have "different" ages on the same calendar day.

Why Leap Years Matter

Every four years, February has 29 days instead of 28. If you were born on February 29 (a "leap day" baby), your age calculation needs special handling. In non-leap years, most calculators treat your birthday as March 1 for celebration purposes — but your true age in days continues to be counted accurately.
