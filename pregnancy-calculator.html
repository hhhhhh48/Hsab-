About the Pregnancy Due Date Calculator

Our free pregnancy calculator estimates your baby's due date based on the first day of your last menstrual period. It uses Naegele's rule, which adds 280 days (40 weeks) to your last period to estimate when your baby will arrive. You will also see your current week of pregnancy, which trimester you are in, and the estimated conception date.

Remember that only about 4% of babies are born exactly on their due date — most arrive within a week before or after. Always consult your doctor for the most accurate assessment, as ultrasound dating may adjust your due date.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the pregnancy due date calculated?

The due date is estimated by adding 280 days (40 weeks) to the first day of your last menstrual period.

How accurate is the due date?

Only about 4% of babies are born on their exact due date. Most births occur within a week before or after.

What are the three trimesters?

The first trimester is weeks 1-13, the second is weeks 14-26, and the third is weeks 27-40.
